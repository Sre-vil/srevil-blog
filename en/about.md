## About

This site is where Srevil records things I have learned or built myself.
Posts are based on my own work and study. When something later turns out to be wrong or incomplete, I update it.

## What I write about

- Methods and setup notes from work I actually tried
- Records of problems I hit, and how I tracked down a cause
- Notes written so I can find the idea again later
