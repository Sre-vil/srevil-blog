Last updated: 2026-11-22

This policy explains how information is handled when you visit the blog at `srevil.com` (the "blog").

## 1. Information collected

The blog has no sign-up or login, and it does not collect personal information that you type in.
Some information may still be processed automatically in the course of hosting the site and showing ads:

- Access logs (IP address, browser type, time of visit, pages viewed, and similar data)
- Information collected through cookies and similar technologies

## 2. Cookies and ads

The blog may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense.

- Third-party advertisers, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's previous visits to this blog and other websites.
- Google may use advertising cookies to serve ads based on visits to this blog and other sites on the internet.
- You can turn off personalized ads in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- You can also opt out of personalized advertising cookies from third parties at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info).
- Google's use of advertising cookies is described in [Google's advertising and privacy policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 3. How to refuse cookies

You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies may stop some features from working as expected.

## 4. Storage and third parties

This blog does not store or sell your personal information itself.
The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process access information as part of hosting. See the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

## 5. External links

Posts may include links to other sites. This blog is not responsible for how those sites handle personal information.

## 6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the law or the service changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.

## 7. Contact

For privacy questions, please open an [issue in the GitHub repository](https://github.com/Sre-vil/srevil-blog/issues/new). Issues are public, so do not include personal information.
